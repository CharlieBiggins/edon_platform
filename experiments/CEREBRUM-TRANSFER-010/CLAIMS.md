# Claims

## Supported now

- The transfer question, candidates, case allocation, baseline classes,
  independence boundary, scope exclusions, metrics, and materialization gates
  are preregistered before DEV-010 scoring.
- The shell can verify that protected transfer artifacts are absent.

## Not supported now

- A DEV-010 pass.
- Candidate registration.
- An independently authored instrument.
- Any prediction, score, transfer result, real-institution result, production
  safety claim, autonomous authority, IFM, or mini-IGI claim.

## Future bounded claim

If every gate passes, the result may support only bounded synthetic EventNet
transfer across the registered independent authorship, generator, renderer,
and governance-form boundaries.

## Limitations

- The planned target remains synthetic and restricted to mechanisms
  representable in the existing Institutional IR.
- Raw-source compilation, explicit representability detection, and learned
  closed-loop operation are excluded.
- Independent people or organizations must supply protected authorship and
  custody; repository code cannot create that independence by declaration.
