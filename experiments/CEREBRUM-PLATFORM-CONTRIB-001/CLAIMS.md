# Claims

## Supported now

- Platform 002 can generate matched active-experience training records.
- The protected target is separately implemented and prompt-disjoint.
- Prediction freezing precedes protected proxy scoring.
- A transparent learned proxy obtains a substantial Platform 002 contribution
  signal with zero unsafe authorizations.
- The Qwen development rehearsal is fully specified and locally preflighted.

## Not supported now

- That Qwen or Cerebrum benefits from Platform 002 experience.
- Independently held transfer or real-institution generalization.
- Causal validity of the generated counterfactuals outside registered semantics.
- Production safety, autonomous institutional operation, or IGI.

The Qwen/Cerebrum claim remains blocked until a fresh independently custodied
target is registered, all four adapter runs are complete, predictions are
frozen, and the custodian performs the protected score transaction.
