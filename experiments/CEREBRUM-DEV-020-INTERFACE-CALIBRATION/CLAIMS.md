# DEV-020 claim boundary

DEV-020 may establish that one direct representation is empirically noninferior
to the raw renderer for this frozen model and that authenticated certificate
fields are preserved at the registered rate on one synthetic heldout split.

It cannot establish:

- that DEV-019 identified a unique internal causal defect;
- that Cerebrum learned a new capability;
- that the selected representation will remain optimal after training;
- independent transfer, real-world safety, autonomous authority, production
  readiness, or IGI.
