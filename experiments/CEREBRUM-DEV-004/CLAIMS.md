# Claims

## Supported

- Seed 1 passes the complete gate.
- Seed 2 preserves the transition, queue, pair, schema, renderer, generation,
  and zero-unsafe requirements but fails one appeal-mechanism floor.

## Not supported

- A passed two-seed result, independent transfer, or production safety.

## Provisional

- None. The bounded disposition is a completed development hold.

## Known limitations

- Same-program synthetic validation. The failed appeal cases are exposed and
  prohibited from successor training or rescoring.
