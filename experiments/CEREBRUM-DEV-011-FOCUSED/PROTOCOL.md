# CEREBRUM-DEV-011-FOCUSED protocol

Registered September 5, 2026 after observing the DEV-010 seed-2 failure. This
is therefore an adaptive development diagnostic, not a confirmatory test.

The only trained and scored task is `CERTIFICATE` at the appeal-resolution
decision-clock boundary. Both frozen DEV-010 adapters are continued under the
same fresh training corpus and independently scored on the same frozen fresh
validation instrument.

Per-run pass requirements are exact raw and compiled schema validity, at least
95% overall decision and semantic accuracy, at least 95% accuracy on both the
resolved `ALLOW` and unresolved `CONTESTED` sides, at least 90% exact paired
boundaries, zero unsafe authorizations, and zero generation-limit hits. Both
parent continuations must pass.

The protocol does not measure retention of transitions, queues, pair contrasts,
invariance, contextual domination, or non-appeal mechanisms. Passing does not
authorize protected transfer.
