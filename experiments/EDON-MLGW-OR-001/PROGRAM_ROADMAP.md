# Program roadmap

## Stage 0 — Public event capture

Archive time-stamped official snapshots and preserve source bytes by hash.
Construct only coarse outage-curve and public-resource records. Status:
`READY_TO_CAPTURE`; no operational comparison is authorized.

## Stage 1 — Partner data readiness

Obtain authorization, data-use terms, security review, custodian appointment,
field dictionaries, retention rules, and authoritative exports. Complete
coverage, temporal-ordering, topology-consistency, and missingness audits.

## Stage 2 — Independent simulator calibration

A team separate from the Cerebrum team reconstructs the discrete-event
restoration environment. Calibrate travel, repair, discovery, tree-clearance,
materials, switching, and energization processes on development storms. Freeze
calibration before protected storms are opened.

## Stage 3 — Protected historical replay

Freeze all six controller conditions, execute label-independent schedules, run
matched uncertainty replicas, and perform one custodied score transaction. At
least three storms are required for the stretch tier.

## Stage 4 — Prospective live shadow

MLGW operates normally. The shadow system receives the registered live feed and
produces recommendations that cannot execute. Preserve actual and shadow
decisions before outcomes are observed. Advance only if Stage 3 passes.

## Stage 5 — Controlled assistance

Begin with damage-assessment priority, crew staging, material positioning, and
ETA forecasting. Each recommendation requires operator review. Switching
recommendations remain a later, separately authorized safety study.

## Stage 6 — Replication

An external team reconstructs or executes the frozen protocol. Publication
reports negative, null, and positive results without rewriting earlier stages.
