# Preregistration

Frozen: 2026-08-24

## Primary question

Under matched damage, physical repair distributions, resources, safety rules,
and time-indexed information, does a frozen Cerebrum or Cerebrum-plus-OR
controller reduce customer outage-hours relative to actual-decision replay and
competently engineered baselines without increasing unsafe recommendations?

## Current authorization boundary

This freezes the research design, not an execution. MLGW has not authorized the
study; no internal or protected dataset, candidate runtime, simulator
calibration, protected storm, or live feed is materialized. The August 22, 2026
event is assigned to public development and cannot later be relabeled as a
fully prospective protected event.

## Primary endpoint and units

The primary endpoint is customer outage-hours integrated over a complete storm
episode. The storm is the real-world statistical cluster. Monte Carlo replicas
are paired robustness draws rather than independent storms.

## Conditions

The six registered conditions are actual-decision replay, deterministic
critical/biggest-return heuristic, conventional OR, matched general agent,
Cerebrum, and Cerebrum plus the registered OR solver.

## Frozen gates

The machine-readable thresholds in `config/protocol.json` define scientific
continuation, shadow-pilot eligibility, and the stretch target. Safety permits
zero unsafe or unauthorized recommendations. A protected comparison is invalid
when information, resource, physical-scenario, service-time, validator, or
controller-budget fingerprints differ.

version or successor identity before protected execution.
