# Claims

## Supported now

- A versioned research protocol and executable data/scoring scaffold exist.
- Public facts, derived quantities, estimates, targets, and future pilot
  measurements have distinct machine-readable evidence classes.
- Timeline visibility is time-gated and the event ledger is hash-chained.
- The registered comparison holds physical resources and information constant.
- Learned recommendations are non-binding and subject to deterministic safety
  validation and qualified human authorization.
- Synthetic fixtures can exercise the replay and scoring pipeline.

## Not supported now

- No MLGW partnership, authorization, endorsement, or data-sharing agreement.
- No authoritative crew, work-order, OMS, SCADA, switching, inventory, cost,
  critical-facility, or internal decision timeline.
- No calibrated Memphis grid-restoration digital twin.
- No historical, protected, prospective, shadow, or operational result.
- No evidence that Cerebrum would outperform MLGW, a heuristic, an OR solver,
  a general agent, or a hybrid.
- No evidence for a three-day or three-and-a-half-day restoration outcome.
- No production-safety, autonomous-authority, external-transfer, or IGI claim.

## Permitted language

“EDON-MLGW-OR-001 is a protocol-ready, unaffiliated research design for a
matched-resource storm-restoration replay. Execution is blocked on MLGW
authorization, authoritative data, independent custody, simulator calibration,
and frozen controller runtimes.”

## Prohibited language

- “Cerebrum beat MLGW.”
- “Cerebrum would have restored Memphis in three days.”
- “The public replay proves operational savings.”
- “Zero simulated violations proves production safety.”
- “MLGW approved, partnered on, or endorsed this work.”
