# Institutional IR mapping

The program maps utility restoration into the existing EDON mechanism tuple
without asserting that the mapping is complete or operationally validated.

| Institutional mechanism | Storm-restoration representation |
| --- | --- |
| Authority | crew qualifications, switching authority, operator approval, mutual-aid scope |
| Evidence | outage inference, damage report, field confirmation, weather and access observations |
| Policy | critical-service priority, safety rules, restoration procedure, escalation rules |
| Time | event time, availability time, effective interval, shift and deadline |
| Workflow | detect, assess, clear access, stage material, repair, validate, energize |
| Resources | crews, vehicles, tree teams, inventory, depots, communications and road access |
| Conflict | competing critical priorities, scarce crews/materials, inconsistent reports |
| Consequence | customers and critical services restored, outage-hours, delay, cost, safety state |

The learned layer may rank, assign, stage, or recommend replanning. Deterministic
components retain topology checks, resource reservation, exact-request
authorization, safety validation, and state commitment.

Source-grounded domain experts must review every field, state transition,
constraint, and abstraction before protected replay. A convenient mapping is
not evidence that the Institutional IR captures the real MLGW institution.
