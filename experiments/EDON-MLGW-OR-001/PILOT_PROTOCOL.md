# Live shadow and controlled-assistance protocol

## Entry requirements

- protected historical replay meets the registered shadow-pilot threshold;
- all safety and authorization gates pass;
- MLGW approves the data feed, staffing, retention, and incident plan;
- controller, prompts, retrieval, OR solver, thresholds, and fallback behavior
  are frozen;
- operators are trained that recommendations are non-binding.

## Shadow mode

For every decision opportunity, record:

1. the exact time-gated state shown to each condition;
2. actual operational decision before outcome;
3. shadow recommendation, abstention, confidence, and provenance;
4. deterministic feasibility and safety-validator output;
5. operator review without requiring adoption;
6. later outcome and adjudicated counterfactual evaluability.

The shadow system cannot dispatch, switch, energize, reserve inventory, message
crews, or update operational state.

## Controlled assistance sequence

1. ETA forecasting and uncertainty communication.
2. Damage-assessment ordering.
3. Crew and equipment staging.
4. Material positioning and prerequisite alerts.
5. Advisory repair sequencing.
6. Switching analysis only under a new protocol with qualified engineering
   review and deterministic power-system validation.

Advancement is additive. Failure at one stage does not authorize bypassing it.
