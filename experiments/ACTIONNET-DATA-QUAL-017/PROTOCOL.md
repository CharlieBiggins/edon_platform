# Protocol

- Generate 384 fresh training records from semantic families 900--903.
- Allocate 48 pivotal pairs across delayed evidence (16), approval withdrawal
  (12), evidence expiry (8), jurisdiction shift (6), and unresolved appeal
  (6).
- Represent every pair as a complete causal chain: queue execution, exact
  state mutation, decision/certificate, and paired causal comparison.
- Use three training renderers and distinct development and confirmation
  renderers.
- Freeze 64 development-selection records from families 920--921.
- Reserve 192 single-use confirmation records from families 940--943.
- Exclude all DEV-014, DEV-015, and DEV-016 validation cases from training.
- Require byte-deterministic generation, exact independent reference engines,
  disjoint cases/pairs/families, and binding_authority=false.
