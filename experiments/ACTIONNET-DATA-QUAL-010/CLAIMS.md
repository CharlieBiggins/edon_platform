# Claims

## Supported after qualification

- Deterministic generation and exact agreement of the retained reference
  schedulers and transition engines.
- Fresh training and validation namespaces, semantic families, renderers, and
  lineages relative to RB1.
- Explicit, executable queue integrity and appeal-boundary targets.
- A label-bearing internal development corpus suitable for the separately
  identified `CEREBRUM-DEV-010` campaign.

## Not supported

- A learned repair result, transfer, independent construction, external or
  real-institution validity, production safety, autonomous authority, or IGI.
