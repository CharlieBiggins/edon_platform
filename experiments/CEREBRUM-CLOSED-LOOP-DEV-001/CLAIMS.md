# Claim boundary

- EDON has an executable synthetic development environment for multi-cycle
  institutional operation.
- The generated package contains 288 training and 72 held-out development
  episodes with explicit failure recovery and verification.
- Deterministic reference replay establishes that the environment is solvable
  and that its proposal/commit boundary functions as registered.
- The data are authorized for internal development training only.

## Not supported

- No C1 model has been trained or scored on this package.
- No two-seed learned closed-loop result exists.
- No Transfer-008, independent-transfer, protected-capstone, bounded-IGI,
  real-institution, production-safety, or autonomous-authority claim is made.
- A teacher-forced turn score cannot substitute for interactive episode
  completion.
