# CEREBRUM-MINI-IGI-CAPSTONE-001

Status: `SHELL_READY_PREREQUISITES_UNMET_MATERIALIZATION_UNAUTHORIZED`

This package defines EDON's minimum credible **internal bounded-IGI capstone**.
It asks whether two independently trained and frozen C1 seed lineages can enter
three protected, previously unseen, structurally different synthetic
institutions and complete a governed institutional loop without protected
retraining:

```text
observe -> compile -> estimate state -> form goals -> plan -> allocate
        -> propose/dispatch -> Kernel authorize and commit
        -> monitor outcome -> replan or terminate
```

The three reserved institutional forms are:

1. regional health operations with hierarchical and matrix authority;
2. municipal utility restoration with incident command and emergency
   delegation;
3. a federated research consortium with quorum, multiscope, and contested
   authority.

The experiment is an internal, project-authored synthetic capstone. Separate
author, candidate, and scoring roles are required, but this is not external
replication and cannot establish universal IGI, real-institution validity, or
production safety.

## What exists now

- a frozen 72-episode, 36-pair reservation;
- all 13 Scope-001 task families, 18 mechanism families, and 14 variation axes;
- nine matched controller conditions, including fair learned baselines and
  same-seed C1-only ablations;
- absolute safety and competence gates, per-institution floors, two-seed
  reproducibility, and a single-score rule;
- schemas, a fail-closed readiness validator, and focused tests.

## What does not exist now

No protected institution, episode, prompt, label, generator, oracle, runtime,
prediction, scorer, score, trained capstone model, or bounded-IGI result is
present. Materialization remains blocked until RB1, Transfer-008, and a
two-seed learned closed-loop development program pass and are frozen.
