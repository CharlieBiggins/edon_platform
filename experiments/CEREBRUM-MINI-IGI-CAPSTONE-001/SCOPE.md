# Frozen capstone scope

## Claim tested

Within the finite `COORDINATED_OPERATIONS_V1` profile, can a frozen Cerebrum
System use the same learned C1 substrate to reason and operate coherently across
three unseen institutional forms, with no institution-specific gradient update
and with every binding transition controlled by Kernel?

Passing may support only this internal statement:

> Two frozen C1 seed lineages produced a reproducible internal bounded
> institutional-intelligence signal across the registered protected synthetic
> capstone, including unseen-institution reasoning and learned closed-loop
> operation under deterministic authority control.

Passing does not prove universal institutional intelligence, all of
`EDON-IGI-SCOPE-001`, real-institution validity, external replication,
production safety, autonomous authority, or applicability to arbitrary human
institutions.

## Institutional-form stress

The three instruments must be authored after candidate freeze by distinct
internal author roles. They must differ in domain, authority topology,
escalation semantics, resource structure, workflow shape, and failure modes.

| Reserved form | Structural stress |
| --- | --- |
| Regional health operations | hierarchy plus matrix authority, scarce shared resources, policy conflict, operational escalation |
| Municipal utility restoration | incident command, emergency delegation, revocation, delayed observations, restoration dependencies |
| Federated research consortium | quorum decisions, multiscope jurisdiction, contested authority, negotiated constraints, federation escalation |

Clinical decisions, continuous physical grid control, unrestricted cyber
actions, and direct learned actuation are out of scope.

## Coverage

The capstone covers all task, mechanism, and variation families registered in
`EDON-IGI-SCOPE-001`, but at smaller finite bounds. Coverage means the protected
instrument contains scored opportunities for each registered family; it does
not mean that every possible composition or institution has been tested.

The reservation contains 72 episodes: 24 per institutional form, arranged as
12 counterfactual pairs per form. Every condition receives the same frozen
observations, tools, release times, action vocabulary, context limits, and
episode ordering.

## Compiler--reasoner isolation

The capstone reports three linked tracks rather than one opaque end-to-end
number:

1. `SOURCE_TO_IR_COMPILER` scores whether the Institution Compiler faithfully
   represents protected sources against a custodian-held reference IR;
2. `GOLD_IR_REASONING_ISOLATION` supplies that reference IR to the frozen
   reasoning controller, isolating C1 transfer from compiler error;
3. `END_TO_END_COMPILED_IR_CLOSED_LOOP` uses only the candidate-compiled IR and
   measures the complete loop.

The hidden reference IR is evaluation material and is never available to the
end-to-end controller. A capstone pass requires both compiler fidelity and
end-to-end competence. Gold-IR reasoning cannot compensate for a failed
compiler gate.

## Adaptation boundary

- zero protected gradient updates;
- zero protected labels, evaluator comments, or answer-bearing metadata before
  the single scoring transaction;
- institution-local context and memory are permitted only within an episode;
- memory is reset between protected episodes;
- no cross-institution memory or case-specific prompt repair;
- no automatic weight update;
- all candidate and baseline revisions are frozen before instrument
  materialization.
