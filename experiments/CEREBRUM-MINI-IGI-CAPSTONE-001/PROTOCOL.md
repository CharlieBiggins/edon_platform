# Protocol

## Sequence

1. Complete and freeze two passing RB1 seed lineages.
2. Execute Transfer-008 once under its frozen fair-baseline amendment and
   require both seeds to pass.
3. Train and freeze a two-seed learned closed-loop successor using development
   environments only.
4. Register immutable candidate, base-model, prompt-pack, tool, and baseline
   hashes in this package.
5. Assign three distinct internal instrument-author roles and a separate
   scorer role; freeze content commitments before protected materialization.
6. Materialize the three protected institutions and run overlap audits against
   every development, transfer, and prompt source.
7. Freeze a custodian reference IR for compiler scoring, then execute the
   source-to-IR, gold-IR reasoning, and end-to-end compiled-IR tracks. The
   reference IR remains unavailable to the end-to-end controller.
8. Execute all nine conditions under matched resources and no protected
   feedback.
9. Freeze predictions, execute the single scorer transaction, and evaluate all
   absolute, comparative, per-institution, and two-seed gates.

## Learned closed loop

At each cycle, the controller receives only evidence whose
`available_to_controller_at` time is not later than the decision time. C1 may
estimate state, form goals, decompose plans, allocate resources, select tools,
dispatch agents, forecast outcomes, and propose recovery. C1 never creates
binding authority.

Kernel checks exact request identity, authority, jurisdiction, policy,
preconditions, resources, and transition validity. Only a Kernel-authorized
commit may change simulator state. The simulator emits outcomes, delayed
observations, conflicts, and failures; Cerebrum must monitor and replan or
terminate safely.

## Matched resources

All eligible learned conditions receive identical observations, release times,
task instructions, schema constraints, deterministic compiler, tool schemas and
results, context and generation limits, hardware class, episode order, and zero
retry budget. Baseline-specific hidden tools or privileged state are prohibited.

## Instrument custody

The protected generator, oracle, prompts, labels, and scorer do not belong in
this shell. Their authors may not inspect candidate predictions before the
single scoring transaction. Candidate developers may not inspect protected
semantic families, labels, oracle code, or scorer code before freeze.

## Evidence tier

A passing result would combine protected multidomain generalization and learned
closed-loop operation internally. It would be stronger than Transfer-008 alone
but remain below real-institution validation and external replication.
