# Materialization handoff

Materialization is currently unauthorized.

The following must all be content-addressed and registered before authorization:

- passing RB1, Transfer-008, and two-seed closed-loop prerequisite summaries;
- two frozen candidate manifests and immutable C1 generation hashes;
- the exact base-model revision, baseline prompt pack, compiler, tool pack, and
  runtime image;
- three distinct institution-author assignments and their generator, input,
  label, and oracle commitments;
- a separate scorer assignment and scorer commitment;
- a complete prohibited-reuse and semantic-overlap audit.

Authorization permits protected instrument construction only. It does not
authorize predictions, scoring, publication, deployment, network delivery, or
binding institutional action. Those remain separate governed events.
