# Scaling Architecture

ActionNet-005 keeps the bounded, deterministic simulation architecture of ActionNet-004: independent scheduling and transition implementations, canonical receipts, explicit lineage registries, and reproducible seeded generation.

The repair corpus scales supervision quality rather than simulation size. It increases transition and queue learning weight, shortens output schemas, and moves mechanical serialization and hashing into an auditable compiler. No billion-agent, real-institution, distributed-production, or autonomous-operation claim is made.
