# Protocol

- Conditions: unmodified base and both frozen DEV-002 seeds.
- Instrument: 300 label-free cases.
- Boundary: new families, renderers, institution domains, and six mechanism
  compositions.
- Runtime: no labels, scorer, training code, retrieval, or prompt tuning.
- Gate: both candidate gates plus base improvements, seed agreement, custody, and
  no post-result tuning.
