# Data design

The repair changes the sampling distribution, timing profiles, renderers, and
lineages without changing the deterministic appeal semantics. A resolution at
or before disposition closes the appeal and permits ordinary evaluation. A
resolution after disposition is deferred, leaving the appeal contested.

`UNRESOLVED_APPEAL` appears four times in each fifteen-position pivotal
schedule. Every focused pair contributes resolved and unresolved certificates,
transition targets, queue traces, and a pair contrast. Certificate weights are
increased within each decision class and then decision-normalized, preserving
overall decision balance while emphasizing the observed failure mode.

The held-out validation contains 64 appeal certificates: 32 resolved `ALLOW`
and 32 unresolved `CONTESTED`. It is never used for training or model selection.
