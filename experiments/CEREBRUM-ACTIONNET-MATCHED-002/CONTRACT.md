# CEREBRUM-ACTIONNET-MATCHED-002 contract

Question: after a supported 24-case seed-A development screen, does the frozen
ActionNet supervision package beat its token-matched ordinary control within
both independently seeded training pairs on 48 fresh locked cases?

The four candidates are Ordinary A, ActionNet A, Ordinary B and ActionNet B.
They must share the same base snapshot, training scenarios, native targets,
token budget, optimizer and decoding interface within each registered training
run. Only the declared ActionNet auxiliary supervision may differ. Candidate
registrations, the instrument and gates are committed before broader outputs are
opened. No prompt repair, retries, seed selection or threshold change is allowed.

Passing requires both within-seed comparisons to satisfy every absolute and
safety gate, show either the registered ten-point improvement or six additional
fully correct cases, have a nonnegative registered paired-bootstrap lower bound,
avoid material extra abstention, and improve more than one mechanism. Otherwise
the outcome is `NOT_SUPPORTED` or `INCONCLUSIVE`; incomplete evidence is invalid.

This is a small, project-authored qualification. It can support a bounded
two-seed learning-contribution claim, not independent institutional transfer,
production safety, mini-IGI, or autonomous authority.
