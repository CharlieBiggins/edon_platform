# Institutional IR

The Institutional IR is the canonical typed representation joining authority,
policy, evidence, time, workflow, resources, conflict, jurisdiction, and
consequences.

## Design properties

- explicit types and schema versions;
- stable identifiers separated from model-facing text;
- six-state semantics: true, false, unknown, missing, contested, invalid;
- deterministic transition contracts;
- source and transformation provenance;
- conflict preservation;
- non-authoritative learned annotations;
- reversible versioned updates.

The current research supports this representation only within registered finite
mechanism classes. It is not claimed to express every institution or legal rule.
