# Sanitized candidate invention families

These are invention-harvesting candidates, not conclusions that the subject
matter is novel, non-obvious, eligible, enabled, owned, or patentable. Detailed
conception records and claim drafts belong in the private counsel workspace.

## EDON-IP-FAM-001 — Institution Compiler and typed Institutional IR

Potential technical center:

- representation of normative, operational, and behavioral inputs as distinct
  evidence layers;
- conflict-preserving reconciliation rather than silent normalization;
- closed typed references across authority, evidence, policy, workflow,
  resources, time, conflict, and consequence;
- deterministic composition, validation, typed diagnostics, and promotion;
- versioned execution with review provenance and rollback.

Public implementation anchors:

- `docs/architecture/institution-compiler.md`
- `docs/architecture/institutional-ir.md`
- `schemas/institution/`
- `src/edon/compiler/`
- `src/edon/ir/`

Do not claim that every natural-language institution can be compiled or that the
IR is a complete universal ontology.

## EDON-IP-FAM-002 — ActionNet governed institutional experience

Potential technical center:

- generation of typed institutional trajectories and causal counterfactuals;
- immutable parent/child lineage and deterministic replay verification;
- separation of authoring, review, protected exposure, qualification, and
  training eligibility;
- tenant-local experience custody with revalidated promotion into a global
  registry that retains normalized abstractions and source commitments rather

- overlap, contamination, source-rights, and evidence-class controls;

## EDON-IP-FAM-003 — Cerebrum System, C1, and Kernel governed operation

Potential technical center:

- a learned controller restricted to typed, non-binding proposals;
- deterministic separation of observed, reported, inferred, authorized, and
  committed institutional state;
- C1 as a replaceable learned layer within a broader state, memory, planning,
  optimization, coordination, prediction, and provenance system;
- horizontal content-bound lineage across source, state, inference, plan,
  optimization, authorization, commit, outcome, and experience;
- rejection of model-generated authority claims rather than accepting or
  repairing them;
- proposal binding to context, model lineage, requested action, and state;
- exact-request, expiring, replay-resistant authorization tokens;
- deterministic commit authority, transactional delivery, immutable shadow
  custody, outcome monitoring, and governed replanning;
- a typed institutional environment boundary that separates agent, system, and
  resource observation from Kernel-authorized action delivery and routes
  reconciled outcomes into governed experience custody;
- separation between learning candidates and authorized weight updates;
- source-diverse detection and reconstruction of cross-system institutional
  cascades caused by poisoned or compromised state; and
- non-binding containment, degraded-operation, and recovery proposals coupled
  to exact-request Kernel authorization and blast-radius constraints.

## Concepts not ready as standalone families

The following currently describe research objectives rather than adequately
reviewed standalone inventions:

- universal or bounded IGI as an abstract capability;
- cross-institution transfer as a result without a specific enabled method;
- generic resource allocation or multi-agent coordination;
- generic cryptographic auditing;
- generic simulation-to-reality validation;
- generic institutional resilience, critical-infrastructure protection, or ASI
  defense without a specific enabled and evaluated technical mechanism;
- industry-specific use of EDON without a distinct technical mechanism.
