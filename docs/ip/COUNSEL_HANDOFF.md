# Counsel handoff and execution plan

## Attorney package

Provide counsel through a private controlled channel:

- this sanitized IP-governance package;
- completed private invention disclosures for selected families;
- human conception and contribution records;
- ownership, employment, contractor, funding, and assignment records;
- prior-public-disclosure inventory with preserved copies and exact dates;
- source, model, data, and dependency license inventory;
- architecture drawings, schemas, source anchors, tests, and negative results;
- private patent/trade-secret boundary decisions;
- preliminary prior-art element maps;
- target jurisdictions, publication schedule, commercial priorities, and budget.

Do not email secrets, signing credentials, or unredacted protected data unless
counsel has approved the channel.

## Questions for counsel

1. Which candidate combinations are plausibly eligible, novel, and non-obvious?
2. Is one integrated filing or multiple initial applications preferable?
3. Which technical details are required for support and which should remain
   secret?
4. What prior disclosures affect United States or foreign rights?
5. Who are the correct inventors for each contemplated claim set?
6. Is ownership clean, and which assignments or consents are missing?
7. What continuation, international, defensive-publication, and trade-secret
   strategy fits the commercial plan?
8. Which proposed marks are clear enough to adopt and file?
9. What copyright registrations and customer-data provisions should be
   prioritized?
10. What release-review record should be retained for future diligence?

## First 30 days

- appoint an internal IP owner and private-record custodian;
- freeze unreviewed enabling disclosures;
- complete the disclosure and ownership inventory;
- hold invention-harvesting interviews;
- choose two or three priority disclosures;
- begin professional prior-art and trademark clearance;
- establish the private trade-secret register;
- audit contributor, model, data, and dependency rights.

## Days 31–60

- complete drawings, embodiments, alternatives, and conception records;
- resolve ownership and contributor gaps;
- decide patent, secret, publication, or defer for each mechanism;
- obtain counsel's filing and foreign-rights strategy;
- prepare filing-quality applications and review technical accuracy;
- create release-review records for planned papers and repositories.

## Days 61–90

- file selected applications when authorized;
- verify receipts, applicants, inventors, ownership, and scope mappings;
- update only sanitized public filing status;
- release approved materials under the publication gate;
- calendar all filing, response, renewal, and publication deadlines;
- review the portfolio after major research or implementation changes.
