# EDON layered IP strategy

## Objective

Protect concrete EDON technical mechanisms without overstating scientific
results, disclosing performance-critical secrets unnecessarily, or confusing a
patent filing with evidence of institutional general intelligence.

## Initial portfolio shape

The first counsel review should consider three integrated candidate packages:

1. **Institution Compiler and Institutional IR** — typed, conflict-preserving
   reconciliation and deterministic institutional execution semantics.
2. **ActionNet governed experience** — causal experience generation,
   counterfactual lineage, replay, qualification, exposure, and eligibility
   controls, including tenant-local custody and rights-bound promotion into
   offline global training releases.
3. **Cerebrum System, C1, and Kernel governed operation** — separated
   observed/reported/inferred/authorized/committed state, non-binding C1
   proposals, horizontal provenance, independently validated optimization,
   exact-request authorization, deterministic commitment, monitoring,
   replanning, and auditable delivery.

Federated coordination, real-institution calibration, and independently
demonstrated transfer remain follow-on candidates. They should not be presented
as enabled inventions merely because they appear on the research roadmap.

## Selection rule

For each mechanism, record one disposition:

| Disposition | Use when |
| --- | --- |
| Patent candidate | Competitors could observe or independently recreate it, and EDON can describe an enabled technical implementation |
| Trade secret | Value depends on confidential data, parameters, heuristics, operational knowledge, or difficult-to-observe implementation choices |
| Defensive/public disclosure | Publication is more valuable than exclusivity and disclosure will not expose protected evaluations or customer material |
| Copyright/contract only | The asset is primarily expression, documentation, a licensed dependency, data access, or an ownership/right-to-use issue |
| Defer | Implementation, inventorship, ownership, novelty, or evidence is not mature enough for a responsible decision |

No mechanism is assigned to both patent disclosure and continued trade-secret
protection for the same disclosed details.

## Portfolio guardrails

- Protect mechanisms and system interactions, not the slogan “IGI.”
- Distinguish implemented embodiments from aspirational capability claims.
- Draft around concrete state, data structures, validation, authorization,
  replay, and computer-system behavior.
- Preserve alternative embodiments so later claim strategy is not tied to one
  model, renderer, optimizer, storage engine, or deployment topology.
- Conduct novelty and eligibility review separately from freedom-to-operate
  review.
- Record human conception contributions; job title and code authorship alone do
  not determine inventorship.
- Confirm ownership and assignment before filing.
- File or deliberately waive filing before detailed enabling publication.
- File additional material before publishing improvements not supported by an
  earlier application.
- Keep protected benchmarks, customer inputs, model weights, recipes, and
  custodian-only artifacts outside public repositories.

## Scientific boundary

The IP portfolio may describe systems that are implemented and testable. It may
not convert the following current gaps into supported claims:

- independent transfer is not established;
- protected multi-domain generalization is not tested;
- learned closed-loop operation is not tested;
- real-institution validity and production safety are not established;
- external replication and bounded formal guarantees are not established.

Patent status and research evidence must remain separate fields in every public
record.
