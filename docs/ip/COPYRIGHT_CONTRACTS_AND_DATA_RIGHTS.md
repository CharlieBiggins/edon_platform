# Copyright, contracts, ownership, and data rights

## Copyright inventory

Track authorship and ownership for:

- source code, tests, SDKs, interfaces, and build tooling;
- papers, documentation, diagrams, web content, and demonstrations;
- original selection, arrangement, and expression in datasets where applicable;
- audiovisual, graphic, and training materials.

Copyright status does not establish ownership of the underlying algorithm,
method, fact, or idea. Registration strategy should be reviewed separately for
commercially significant releases.

## Contributor ownership

For every founder, employee, contractor, researcher, advisor, or joint developer,
record:

- legal identity and engagement type;
- agreement effective date;
- confidentiality obligations;
- present assignment of relevant inventions and other IP;
- work-product and copyright ownership;
- pre-existing inventions and excluded materials;
- continuing cooperation for filings;
- conflicting employer, university, grant, sponsor, or customer obligations;
- offboarding and return/deletion confirmation.

Do not infer patent inventorship from title, employment, repository access, or
code authorship. Record the human conception contribution to each claimed
mechanism separately.

Signed agreements and personal information must remain outside the public
repository. The public rights register records only verification status.

## Customer and partner data terms

Pilot and commercial agreements should address at minimum:

- customer ownership and permitted processing of raw data;
- minimization, privacy, security, retention, deletion, and incident response;
- EDON's rights, if any, to deidentified or aggregated derived information;
- rights to create simulations, evaluations, and institutional experience;
- whether and how customer-derived information may influence global models;
- ownership of customer-specific configurations and general platform
  improvements;
- restrictions on re-identification, cross-customer use, publication, and model
  training;
- audit, export, termination, transition, and deletion obligations;
- regulated-data and jurisdiction-specific requirements.

No dataset becomes training-eligible merely because EDON can technically access
it. Rights, privacy, scientific custody, provenance, and qualification gates all
remain required.

## Dependency and model hygiene

Maintain a software and model bill of materials containing:

- component, version, source, and hash;
- license and notice obligations;
- modification and distribution status;
- copyleft, network-use, research-only, non-commercial, field-of-use, or model
  output restrictions;
- dataset and weight provenance;
- compatibility decision and reviewer.

Avoid copying code, data, prompts, benchmarks, papers, or weights without a
recorded right to use them. Third-party licenses remain separate from EDON's
repository notice.

## Filing ownership gate

Before any patent filing, counsel must verify:

- correct human inventors;
- chain of title or planned assignments;
- company/entity identity;
- funding and joint-development obligations;
- third-party material and license exposure;
- whether any institution, employer, sponsor, customer, or government body may
  possess rights.
