# Trademark and brand plan

This is a clearance-first planning record, not a trademark availability opinion
or registration claim.

| Role | Candidate | Current public status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Company/master brand | EDON | Clearance required; unregistered in this repository |
| Learned system | CEREBRUM | Clearance required; crowded/common-word risk requires particular care |
| Experience platform | ACTIONNET | Clearance required |
| Category term | Institutional General Intelligence / IGI | Treat primarily as descriptive category language |
| Governance component | EDON KERNEL | Evaluate as a combined source identifier, not ownership of “kernel” generally |

## Symbol rules

- Do not use the registered-trademark symbol unless registration is verified for
  the relevant mark and goods or services.
- Use plain text by default while clearance is pending.
- Use `TM` only under the approved brand policy; it does not represent federal
  registration.
- Record country, owner, application/registration number, classes, goods or
  services, first-use evidence, and renewal deadlines before changing status.

## Clearance workflow

1. Define the actual goods and services for the next three years.
2. Search exact, phonetic, visual, translated, and conceptually similar marks.
3. Review relevant registries, common-law use, companies, products, domains,
   applications, publications, and app stores.
4. Obtain counsel's confusion-risk assessment.
5. Decide file, modify, defer, or abandon.
6. Preserve approved specimens and first-use evidence.
7. Monitor confusing use and maintain renewal/deadline records.

Domain-name or company-name availability does not establish trademark
availability.

## Category strategy

EDON may encourage use of “Institutional General Intelligence” as a research
category while using EDON, Cerebrum, and ActionNet as source identifiers,
subject to clearance. Scientific category adoption and trademark exclusivity
should not be conflated.

## Current restriction

No mark in the public registry is recorded as federally registered. Repository
documentation must therefore not render EDON, Cerebrum, or ActionNet with the
registered-trademark symbol.
