# Claim boundaries

## Currently supportable

- heterogeneous institutional mechanisms can share a typed representation within
  the registered finite mechanism classes;
- counterfactual ActionNet supervision provides measurable learnable signal on
  the completed synthetic development task;
- registered synthetic repair failures can be reproducibly reduced internally;
- partial signal crossed one separately implemented synthetic boundary;
- `EDON-IGI-SCOPE-001` defines a finite environment class for future bounded
  generality evaluation;
- the repository implements an internal Cerebrum System reference composition
  with C1, separated state projection, and horizontal provenance;
- the repository implements an internal local-to-global ActionNet governance
  reference with offline C1 version custody.
- `CEREBRUM-PLATFORM-VISION-001` freezes a coherent target architecture and six
  canonical specifications without claiming implementation or qualification.
- `CEREBRUM-PLATFORM-VISION-002` additively freezes the Institutional Control
  Graph, Delegated Authority and Commitment Layer, and receipt model without
  claiming their implementation or qualification.
- `CEREBRUM-MATURE-PLATFORM-SPEC-001` freezes the eleven-specification mature
  product and technical-contract map without claiming implementation,
  qualification, customer value, or bounded institutional intelligence.
- `CEREBRUM-PLATFORM-FOUNDATION-001` registers vendor-neutral implementation
  requirements and freeze gates without claiming that any service or control
  has been built or qualified.
- `CEREBRUM-AWS-DEPLOYMENT-PROFILE-001` registers a replaceable proposed AWS
  mapping without claiming deployed infrastructure, fixed cost, compliance,
  customer authorization, or production qualification.

## Not currently supportable

- general transfer across independently authored real institutions;
- automatic compilation of natural institutional sources;
- production safety or autonomous authority;
- a universal institutional ontology;
- machine-checked correctness of the complete system;
- a demonstrated institutional learning/scaling law;
- superiority to matched live systems or expert teams.
- implementation, protected qualification, production readiness, or customer
  value of the complete Cerebrum platform vision;
- a deployed Control Plane, Execution Assurance service, Deployment Controller,
  public v1 institutional-task API, or production Command Center;
- a frozen or qualified Platform Foundation, deployed AWS reference profile,
  measured production SLO, executed healthcare agreement, or regulated-data
  processing authorization;
- an implemented or validated Institutional Control Graph, Mandate Service,
  Commitment Ledger, Resource Reservation System, Decision Receipt Service, or
  Compensation Manager;
- an implemented or validated Model and Solver Registry, enforceable routing
  policy, model invocation receipt service, purpose-bound model data-access
  service, or mature ActionNet learning pipeline;
- a production Institutional Environment Gateway or verified causal attribution
  from captured actions to institutional outcomes;
- learned performance of the complete Cerebrum System or automatic correctness
  of inferred institutional state;
- improved C1 performance, a learning scaling law, or privacy guarantees from
  the existence of the learning-network implementation.
- a passing result from `CEREBRUM-MINI-IGI-CAPSTONE-001`; the package currently
  contains only a frozen reservation, gates, schemas, and readiness controls;
- bounded IGI merely because the capstone covers all registered task,
  mechanism, and variation families; no protected institutions, predictions,
  or scores exist yet;
- validated detection or containment of AI-enabled institutional attacks,
  poisoned state, cross-system cascades, latent adversarial coordination, or
  resource-exhaustion campaigns;
- protection of critical infrastructure, national-security operation, or
  defense against artificial superintelligence.

The existence of an IGI scope definition is not evidence that Cerebrum covers
the scope. Transfer-008 may test only the registered EventNet Core slice.

Readiness, schemas, and successful transparent rules ceilings are infrastructure,
not learned-model evidence.

The frozen platform vision is architecture governance. Its diagrams,
specifications, schemas, product package, and terminology do not establish that
the components operate together, that any workflow is qualified, or that EDON
may exercise institutional authority.

Renaming the learned architecture role to C1 does not alter or strengthen any
historical `CEREBRUM-*` result. Historical model-only experiments remain
model-only evidence. The new State Engine and causal/provenance graph are
reference implementations, not evidence of C1 accuracy, causal validity,
closed-loop performance, transfer, or production readiness.

Memory flags, promotion checks, training-release hashes, and C1 version records
are governance infrastructure. They do not establish that any operational
experience is legally reusable, sufficiently de-identified, scientifically
valid, or beneficial for training. No model has been trained through the new
Global ActionNet reference.

The Institutional Environment Gateway currently defines an umbrella
architecture and non-authoritative event contract. Existing components cover
parts of that architecture, but the definition is not evidence of live
agent/system/resource integration, successful closed-loop control, or an IGI
capability.

The institutional-resilience document is a threat model and evidence roadmap.
Policy relevance, architectural coherence, or successful component tests do not
establish adversary resistance, attribution accuracy, safe intervention,
critical-infrastructure protection, government authorization, or a
national-security capability.

`CEREBRUM-MINI-IGI-CAPSTONE-001` is the minimum planned internal conjunction
test for unseen-institution transfer and learned closed-loop operation. Even a
future two-seed pass would authorize only the phrase "internal bounded mini-IGI
signal within Capstone-001." It would not establish universal IGI, all of
Scope-001, real-institution validity, external replication, production safety,
or binding machine authority.
