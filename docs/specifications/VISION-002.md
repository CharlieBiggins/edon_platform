4. [Action Lifecycle](action-lifecycle.md)
5. [Release and Deployment Contract](release-deployment-contract.md)
6. [First Qualified Operational Workflow](first-qualified-operational-workflow.md)

Vision-002 adds:

7. [Institutional Control Graph](institutional-control-graph.md)
8. [Delegated Authority and Commitment Layer](delegated-authority-commitment.md)
9. [Decision, Execution, and Compensation Receipts](decision-execution-receipts.md)

All nine specifications remain target contracts. Their existence does not
establish implementation, qualification, production safety, customer value, or
binding authority.
