# EDON IP governance

Status: `INTERNAL_PROCESS_READY_COUNSEL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`

This is the repository-level entry point for EDON invention, publication,
trade-secret, trademark, copyright, data-rights, and ownership governance.
It is an operational research record, not a patent filing or legal opinion.

EDON has not represented that any patent application has been filed, that any
candidate invention is patentable, or that any mark is registered. Do not use
“patent pending” until a filing receipt and scope mapping are recorded. Do not
use the registered-trademark symbol until registration for the relevant mark
and goods or services is verified.

## Public repository package

- [`docs/ip/`](docs/ip/README.md): strategy, workflows, checklists, and sanitized
  templates.
- [`governance/ip/`](governance/ip/README.md): machine-readable status,
  candidate-family, disclosure, brand, secrecy, and release-review records.
- [`scripts/release/validate_ip_governance.py`](scripts/release/validate_ip_governance.py):
  deterministic validation of the public IP-governance package.

## Private material

Do not commit invention drafts prepared for counsel, legal advice, unfiled
enabling details designated confidential, contributor agreements, signatures,
customer terms, protected benchmarks, source data, or trade-secret values.
Private records should live in a separately controlled system. The public
repository may retain only a sanitized identifier, status, custodian, and hash
after review.

## Required sequence before a detailed public release

1. Inventory prior disclosures and affected invention families.
2. Record human conception contributions and verify assignment status.
3. Complete novelty, eligibility, ownership, license, and secrecy review.
4. Obtain counsel direction and, when applicable, a filing receipt.
5. Map every released technical detail to filed or intentionally public matter.
6. Pass scientific-claim and protected-evaluation release gates independently.

The IP process never upgrades EDON's scientific evidence. A patent filing would
not establish transfer, closed-loop performance, safety, IGI, or production
readiness.
