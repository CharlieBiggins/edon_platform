# Claims

## Supported on pass

- The local gateway normalizes and immutably records the tested protocol data.
- Tenant, replay, allowlist, FHIR write, and authority boundaries behave as
  exercised by the evaluation.
- Gateway receipts remain non-binding and do not claim delivery or execution.

## Not supported

- Native interoperability or certification with any named vendor.
- Production identity, secret, transport, queue, observability, privacy,
  clinical, availability, or incident-response readiness.
- Real-institution safety, performance, savings, or autonomous authority.
