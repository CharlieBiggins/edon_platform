# Protocol

1. Create two independently versioned facility worlds.
2. Register one tenant-isolated global/domain/region/facility hierarchy.
3. Project facility worlds without raw observations or payloads.
4. Aggregate capacity through region, domain, and global levels.
5. Route local, cross-facility, and higher-authority decisions.
6. Record escalation acknowledgement and resolution as additive events.
7. Request a capacity-allocation candidate bound to a projection hash.
8. Verify feasibility, non-binding output, and rejection of authority smuggling.

All twelve checks must pass. Thresholds and cases are internal fixtures and
must not be described as a real logistics validation.
