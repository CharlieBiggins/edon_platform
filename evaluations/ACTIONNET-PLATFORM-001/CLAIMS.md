# Claims

## Supported

- Product records are tenant-isolated, immutable, content-bound, and audited.
- Experiences default to non-authoritative, non-binding, and ineligible for training.
- Protected experiences cannot enter training releases.
- Training eligibility requires distinct review dimensions and an overlap pass.
- Coverage is explicitly limited to a registered matrix.
- Acquisition and intervention outputs remain non-binding candidates.

## Not supported

- Production readiness or external security assurance.
- Learned active acquisition, intervention discovery, or human behavior.
- Real-institution correctness or transferable Cerebrum improvement.
- Automatic training, release, or binding institutional execution.
