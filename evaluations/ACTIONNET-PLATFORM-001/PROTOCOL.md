# Protocol

The evaluation registers two mechanisms and a typed composition edge, creates
and freezes an adversarial procedural institution, records ordinary, protected,
and counterfactual experience, executes distinct domain/safety/lineage/training
reviews, performs a protected-overlap check, creates a training release, records
model exposure, freezes a registered coverage snapshot, recommends new
experience, and creates a non-binding intervention candidate.

All eighteen gates must pass. The evaluation uses deterministic internal
fixtures and cannot be presented as a production or real-institution result.
