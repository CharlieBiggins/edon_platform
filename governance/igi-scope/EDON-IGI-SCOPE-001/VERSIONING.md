# Versioning and anti-retrofit policy

Scope-001 is frozen before Transfer-008 materialization and scoring.

The following changes require a successor identity such as
`EDON-IGI-SCOPE-002`:

- adding or removing mechanism families, tasks, domains, or safety invariants;
- increasing any evaluation-profile bound;
- changing the protected adaptation rules;
- changing what counts as independent transfer;
- weakening exclusions or authorization boundaries;
- changing the generality evidence tiers or promotion gates.

Typographical corrections may be issued as a patch version only when they do
not alter machine-readable semantics. Every patch must update the file inventory
and explain the correction.

Experimental failures do not justify modifying Scope-001 retrospectively. They
may motivate a successor scope or a narrower experimental claim.
