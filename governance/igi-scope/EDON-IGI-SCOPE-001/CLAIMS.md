# Claims

## Supported now

- Scope-001 defines a finite, falsifiable institutional environment class.
- Three nested evaluation profiles and their maximum bounds are frozen.
- Mechanism, task, variation, safety, transfer, budget, and exclusion contracts
  are explicit.
- Transfer-008 is classified as a narrow planned EventNet evidence slice.
- The current evidence coverage matrix records that Scope-001 generality is not
  established.

## Unsupported now

- Cerebrum does not currently cover Scope-001.
- Representation completeness over Scope-001 is not proven.
- Independent transfer, broad multi-domain generalization, learned closed-loop
  operation, real-institution validity, and external replication are not
  established.
- No theorem establishes bounded regret, near-optimality, or safety over every
  environment in Scope-001.
- Universal IGI, unrestricted intelligence, production autonomy, and binding
  learned authority are prohibited claims.

## Permitted language

“EDON-IGI-SCOPE-001 freezes the first bounded institutional environment class
against which future Cerebrum generality evidence may be accumulated.”

The word “IGI” may identify the research scope. It may not be used to describe
an achieved model result.
