# Compiler review protocol

1. Verify source identity, version, license, and hash.
2. Inspect extracted authority, evidence, temporal, workflow, resource, and
   jurisdiction relations.
3. Compare normative, operational, and behavioral layers.
4. Preserve every unresolved conflict.
5. Require domain review for moderate-risk mechanisms and dual review for high or
   critical risk.
6. Approve, reject, or revise the candidate with a signed provenance record.
7. Generate ActionNet qualification cases only after approval.

The compiler's focused review queue is an exception queue, not an approval
shortcut. Candidates absent from that queue still require a recorded authorized
promotion decision before they can enter an institution's live IR registry.
