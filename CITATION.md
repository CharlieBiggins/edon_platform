# Citation

If you use this research software, cite the EDON Research Platform repository
and the specific frozen experiment manifests supporting the result you rely on.

```text
EDON Research Lab. EDON Research Platform, version 0.3.0, 2026.
```

Every scientific citation should additionally identify the experiment ID,
artifact version, result hash, and claim boundary. A repository citation alone
does not convert readiness infrastructure into experimental evidence.
